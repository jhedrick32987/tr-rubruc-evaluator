# Fireteam Forge Public Source Registry

The registry is scoped to sources named in the three project documents, sources directly required to interpret Marine Corps T&R standards, and public policy needed to connect evaluation data to MCTIMS or readiness reporting.

## Relevance tiers

- Tier 1: Direct input or governing method for task-to-rubric generation.
- Tier 2: Interpretive doctrine, governance, or system-integration source.
- Tier 3: Strategic, joint, readiness, or cross-service context. Do not use it to create task-specific anchors unless a Tier 1 source expressly incorporates it.

## Excluded by scope

The thesis bibliography also contains academic literature on BARS reliability, performance measurement, wearables, technology adoption, and organizational learning. Those works are relevant to research design, but they are not Marine Corps publications, doctrine, training manuals, or field manuals. They are listed in the converted thesis proposal under `00_project_context` and were not copied into this government-source corpus.

MOJT

## Condition

Given an MK153 SMAW, a day optical device, target, rocket/weighted encasement, and a magazine of spotting rounds, while wearing a fighting load.

## Standard

To achieve point of aim/point of impact.

## Performance Steps

1. Mount the optic to the MK153 2. Prepare the MK153 SMAW spotting rifle for firing 3. Assume a firing position 4. Fire the spotting rifle at sight specific range 5. Adjust the day optic to the point of impact 6. Repeat steps 4 and 5 until point-of-aim is point of impact 7. Record errors if required

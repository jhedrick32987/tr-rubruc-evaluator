## Condition

## Standard

To return the weapon into action.

## Performance Steps

1. Wait 5 seconds and re-cock mechanism 2. Aim and pull trigger. If a new misfire occurs, keep rifle oriented towards target 3. Re-cock mechanism 4. Attempt to reengage the target 5. Remove round if weapon fails to fire 6. Reload with a new round

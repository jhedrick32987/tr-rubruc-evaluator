# 0341-WPNS-2004 Mount an Aiming Circle

## Evaluation coded

## Initial training setting

FORMAL

## Condition

Given an SL-3 complete aiming circle and a direction of fire, mount an aiming circle ensuring the aiming circle is leveled in all four cardinal directions using the tubular level

## Standard

Ensuring the aiming circle is leveled in all four cardinal directions using the tubular level. (TC 3-22.91, Mortar Fire Direction Procedures, Ch. 3, p. 3-47)

## Performance Steps

1. Setup the aiming circle tripod 2. Open the base plate cover of the aiming circle head 3. Mount the aiming circle to the tripod 4. Level the aiming circle

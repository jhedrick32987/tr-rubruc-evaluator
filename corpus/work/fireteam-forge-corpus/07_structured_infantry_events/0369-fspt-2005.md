## Condition

## Standard

To achieve the desired effects on target(s) that supports the scheme of maneuver.

## Performance Steps

1. Update situation template: a. Location of friendly forces b. Location of enemy forces c. Fire support coordination measures (FSCMs) d. Tactical control measures (TCMs) 2. Refine target information a. Target precedence b. Target location 3. Establish and maintain communication with fire support assets 4. Consider battlespace geometry when employing fires 5. Integrate direct fires into the scheme of maneuver 6. Assess the effects of fires 7. Process battle damage assessment (BDA) reports

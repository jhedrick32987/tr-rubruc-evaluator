# 0341-WPNS-1003 Boresight a Mortar

## Evaluation coded

NO SUSTAINMENT INTERVAL: 6 months

## Readiness coded

NO

## MOS performing

0341

## Billets

Ammunition bearer, Gunner, Squad Leader

## Grades

PVT, PFC, LCPL, CPL, SGT

## Initial training setting

FORMAL

## Condition

Given mortar, mounted in the upper saddle, a boresight, while wearing a fighting load.

## Standard

To align the canon and the sight with a zero tolerance for deflection and elevation. (TC 3-22.90, Mortars, Ch. 2, p. 2-3)

## Performance Steps

1. Install the boresight 2. Identify a distant aiming point 3. Determine the mortar's initial sight setting a. Deflection b. Elevation 4. Remove the boresight

## Primary Reference

TC 3-22.90 Mortars

# 0341-WPNS-2005 Declinate an Aiming Circle

## Evaluation coded

NO SUSTAINMENT INTERVAL: 6 months

## Readiness coded

NO

## Description

The aiming circle is used to measure azimuth and elevation angles with respect to a pre-selected baseline. It is a low-power telescope that is mounted on a composite body and contains a magnetic compass, adjusting mechanisms, and leveling screws for establishing a horizontal plane. The instrument is supported by a base plate for mounting on a tripod. Angular measurements in azimuth are indicated on graduated scales and associated micrometers. (TC 3-22.91, Mortar Fire Direction Procedures, Ch. 3, p. 3-40)

## Description

Immediate actions are designed to provide swift and positive small unit reaction to visual or physical contact to the enemy. They are simple courses of action in which all Marines are well trained. Minimal signals or commands are required and they are developed as needed for the combat situation. Immediate action include, but are not limited to; immediate halt, air observation and/or attack, meeting engagement (hasty ambush, immediate assault), break contact, counter to near and far ambush.

## Condition

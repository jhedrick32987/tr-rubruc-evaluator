# 0300-COND-1001 Conduct a Forced March

## Evaluation coded

NO SUSTAINMENT INTERVAL: 24 months

## Readiness coded

NO

## MOS performing

0300, 0311, 0317, 0331, 0341, 0351, 0352, 8999

## Grades

PVT, PFC, LCPL, CPL, SGT, SSGT, GYSGT, 1STSGT, 2NDLT, 1STLT, CAPT

## Initial training setting

FORMAL

## Condition

Given an assault load of 70 pounds (+/- 10%), assigned weapon and mission essential equipment.

## Standard

To move 20 kilometers in under 5 hours.

## Performance Steps

1. Conduct Pre-Combat Checks (PCCs) 2. Complete 20 kilometer march 3. Conduct follow-on actions

## Initial training setting

FORMAL

## Condition

Given a machinegun unit, ammunition, and an objective while wearing a fighting load.

## Standard

To ensure machinegun fire is distributed over the entire target area. (MCTP 3-01C, Machine Guns and Machine Gun Gunnery, Ch. 6, p. 6-23)

## Performance Steps

1. Scan and observe for potential target(s) 2. Issue fire command using ADDRAC: a. Alert b. Direction c. Description d. Range e. Assignment f. Control 3. Issue subsequent fire commands, to repeat or correct a fire command, to adjust fire, to cease or commence fire, or to terminate the alert

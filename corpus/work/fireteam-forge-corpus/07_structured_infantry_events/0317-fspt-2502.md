# 0317-FSPT-2502 Conduct a Suppression of Enemy Air Defense (SEAD) Fire Mission

## Evaluation coded

NO SUSTAINMENT INTERVAL: 6 months

## Readiness coded

NO

## MOS performing

0317

## Grades

CPL, SGT, SSGT

## Initial training setting

MOJT

## Condition

Given the references, equipment organic to a Forward Observer, a mortar unit, and a Forward Air Controller (FAC) or Joint Terminal Attack Controller (JTAC) if aircraft are used.

## Standard

By transmitting the call-for-fire (CFF) request in the correct sequence.

## Performance Steps

1. Determine target location to suppress 2. Determine target location for the mark 3. Transmit the call for fire request 4. Monitor the timeline for suppression and marking rounds 5. Assess effects of suppression and marking rounds 6. Report to Fire Support Team (FiST) Team, as necessary 7. Transmit (RREMS) a. Refinement b. Record as Target c. End of mission d. Surveillance

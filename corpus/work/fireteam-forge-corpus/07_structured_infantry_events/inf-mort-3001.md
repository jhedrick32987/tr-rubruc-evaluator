## Description

The tempo of battle and the threat of enemy counter-fire mean that mortar platoons and sections may have to move often. To reduce the time spent displacing, a mortar platoon accomplishes the reconnaissance, selection, occupation, and movement tasks quickly and efficiently. The key to a successful reconnaissance, selection, and occupation of position is frequent and effective training. Although automated fire direction systems provide extremely accurate locations and greatly simplify the techniques used to occupy and fire from a new position, mortar leaders must be prepared to conduct operations under analog conditions. (MCTP 3-01D, Tactical Employment of Mortars, Ch. 4)

## Condition

Given an order to establish mortar firing positions.

## Standard

To continue to fire missions while under direct and indirect enemy fire in accordance with determined level of protection, time, and resources available. (MCTP 3-01D, Tactical Employment of Mortars, Ch. 8, p. 8-6)

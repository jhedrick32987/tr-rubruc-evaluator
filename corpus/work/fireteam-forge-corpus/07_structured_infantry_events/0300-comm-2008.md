# 0300-COMM-2008 Operate a Very High Frequency (VHF) Field Radio

## Evaluation coded

NO SUSTAINMENT INTERVAL: 12 months

## Readiness coded

NO

## MOS performing

0300, 0302, 0311, 0317, 0331, 0341, 0351, 0352, 0369

## Grades

PVT, PFC, LCPL, CPL, SGT, SSGT, GYSGT, 2NDLT, 1STLT, CAPT

## Initial training setting

MOJT

## Condition

Given a SL-3 complete VHF radio with a fill, a frequency or net ID, and a distant station, while wearing a fighting load.

## Standard

To establish communication with the distant station.

## Performance Steps

1. Assemble the radio set 2. Load a net ID 3. Conduct a communication's check 4. Troubleshoot, as required

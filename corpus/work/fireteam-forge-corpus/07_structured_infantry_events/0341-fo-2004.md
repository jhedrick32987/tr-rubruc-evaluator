# 0341-FO-2004 Conduct a Coordinated Illumination Mission

## Evaluation coded

NO SUSTAINMENT INTERVAL: 6 months

## Readiness coded

NO

## Description

Coordinated illumination is a type of fire in which the firing of illuminating and HE projectiles is coordinated to provide illumination of the target and surrounding area only at the time required for spotting and adjusting fire. The observer may allow the FDC to control the firing of the illumination and HE rounds by marking the illumination round when it provides optimal illumination on the target, or he may control the firing of each round by using procedures of by round at my command. (MCRP 3-10F.2, Supporting Arms Observer, Spotter and Controller, Ch. 3, p. 3-15)

## Standard

To engage the target(s) with effective fires and obtain desired effects. (MCRP 3-10F.2, Supporting Arms Observer, Spotter and Controller, Ch. 6, p. 6-5)

## Performance Steps

1. Transmit the complete illumination call For fire (CFF), in sequence 2. Transmit subsequent corrections to include height of burst, if required 3. Once target is illuminated, determine target location 4. Transmit "MARK" when the illumination round best illuminates the target 5. Transmit coordinated illumination call For fire (CFF), in sequence 6. Transmit subsequent corrections within 15 seconds of High Explosive (HE) round impact 7. Fire for effect (FFE) 8. Transmit (RREMS) a. Refinement data b. Record as Target c. End of Mission d. Surveillance

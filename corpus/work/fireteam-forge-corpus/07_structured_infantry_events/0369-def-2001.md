# 0369-DEF-2001 Lead a Unit in the Defense (S/L)

## Evaluation coded

NO SUSTAINMENT INTERVAL: 6 months

## Readiness coded

NO

## MOS performing

0369

## Billets

Platoon Sergeant, Section Leader

## Grades

SSGT, GYSGT

## Initial training setting

FORMAL

## Condition

Given a unit, an area to defend, and an order from higher.

## Standard

To defeat enemy attacks or prevent the enemy from achieving its objectives in accordance with the commander's intent.

## Performance Steps

1. Conduct METT-T analysis: a. Mission b. Enemy c. Terrain and weather d. Troops and fire support available e. Time 2. Conduct the troop leading steps (BAMCIS): a. Begin the planning b. Arrange reconnaissance c. Make reconnaissance d. Complete the plan e. Issue the order f. Supervise 3. Rehearse defensive battle drills 4. Establish and supervise alert posture of the defense

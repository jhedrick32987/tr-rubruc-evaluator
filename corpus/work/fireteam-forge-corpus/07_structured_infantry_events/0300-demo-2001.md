# 0300-DEMO-2001 Probe for a Mine

## Evaluation coded

## Initial training setting

MOJT

## Condition

Given an individual weapon, a non-metallic probe, and an emplaced antipersonnel or antitank mine, while wearing a fighting load.

## Standard

To locate a mine without detonation.

## Performance Steps

1. Remove all non-essential equipment 2. Sling rifle across back 3. Assume a prone position 4. Look for trip wires 5. Feel for trip wires 6. Mark and bypass trip wires if located 7. Probe for solid objects 8. Mark and bypass solid objects

# 0341-FO-2009 Conduct an Adjust Fire Mission

## Evaluation coded

NO SUSTAINMENT INTERVAL: 6 months

## Readiness coded

NO

## Description

The adjust-fire mission is requested when the observer decides that an adjustment is needed because of questionable target location or lack of registration corrections. (MCRP 3-10F.2, Supporting Arms Observer, Spotter and Controller, p. Ch 3, pg. 3-2)

## MOS performing

0341

## Billets

Forward Observer

## Grades

LCPL, CPL, SGT

## Initial training setting

FORMAL

## Condition

Provided references equipment organic to the forward observer (FO), a mortar unit, and a threat.

## Standard

To adjust the rounds to have the desired effect on the target, completing a call for fire within 60 seconds of target identification using one of the three target location methods.

## Performance Steps

1. Identify the threat 2. Generate a call for fire (CFF) request 3. Transmit call for fire (CFF) by using the six elements: a. Observer identification b. Warning order NAVMC 3500.44D 07 May 2020

14-26 Enclosure (1) c. Target location d. Target description e. Method of engagement f. Method of fire and control 4. Transmit observer target (OT) direction with or before the first correction 5. Transmit subsequent corrections to within 50 meters of target 6. Enter fire for effect phase 7. Transmit (RREMS) a. Refinement b. Record as Target c. End of Mission d. Surveillance

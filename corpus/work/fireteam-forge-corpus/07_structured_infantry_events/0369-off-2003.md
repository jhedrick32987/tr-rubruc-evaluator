## Administrative Instructions

Logistical considerations for mortar ammunition: the movement of rounds and avoiding excessive breakout is essential. Due to the necessity of a unit to conduct a foot movement in support of this task, mortar ammunition consumption rates must be well planned in order to avoid undue breakout. Special attention must be paid to load plans, and the spread loading of ammunition in support of this movement. {A1268154-5036-019E-E053-C90A2BD6C9EB}*{982DF3EF-E7D7-5B47-E053-D00A2BD6F752}

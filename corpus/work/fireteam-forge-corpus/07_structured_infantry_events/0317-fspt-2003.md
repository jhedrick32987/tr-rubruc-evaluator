## Initial training setting

## Condition

Given equipment organic to a forward observer (FO), mortar unit, a threat area, and the reference.

## Standard

Ensuring the illumination call-for-fire (CFF) is transmitted within 2 minutes and the threat area is illuminated.

## Performance Steps

1. Locate the target 2. Transmit the complete illumination call-for-fire, in sequence 3. Determine subsequent corrections 4. Transmit subsequent corrections 5. Transmit (RREMS) a. Refinement data b. Record as Target (if desired) c. End of Mission d. Surveillance

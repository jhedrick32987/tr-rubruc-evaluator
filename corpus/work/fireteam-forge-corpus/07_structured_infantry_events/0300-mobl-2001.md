# 0300-MOBL-2001 Lead a Team or Squad in a Motorized Patrol (S/L)

## Evaluation coded

NO SUSTAINMENT INTERVAL: 6 months

## Readiness coded

NO

## MOS performing

0311, 0331, 0341, 0351, 0352

## Grades

CPL, SGT

## Initial training setting

MOJT

## Condition

Given a team or squad, motorized assets, rules of engagement, and a mission.

## Standard

To accomplish the mission in accordance with the commander's intent.

## Performance Steps

1. Receive an order 2. Analyze the order with METT-T a. Mission b. Enemy c. Terrain and weather d. Troops and fire support available e. Time 3. Conduct the troop leading steps BAMCIS a. Begin the planning b. Arrange reconnaissance c. Make reconnaissance d. Complete the plan e. Issue the order f. Supervise 4. Prepare vehicles 5. Conduct rehearsals 6. Conduct confirmation brief 7. Execute the motorized patrol 8. Conduct a debrief

# 0300-WPNS-2008 Zero Back-up Iron Sights (BUIS) with the Small Arms Collimator (SAC)

## Evaluation coded

NO SUSTAINMENT INTERVAL: 12 months

## Readiness coded

NO

## MOS performing

0302, 0311, 0331, 0341, 0351, 0352, 0365, 0369

## Initial training setting

MOJT

## Condition

Given a Small Arms Collimator (SAC) that has passed the confidence checker, a weapon with Back-up Iron Sights (BUIS), references, and another Marine.

## Standard

To achieve a personal zero position.

## Performance Steps

1. Ensure the weapon is Condition-4 2. Remove optic 3. Install back-up iron sights (BUIS) 4. Adjust rear sight elevation to 200 meters 5. Insert mandrel into the barrel NAVMC 3500.44D 07 May 2020

8-81 Enclosure (1) 6. Adjust rear sight windage and front sight post to the I,9 location in the Small Arms Collimator (SAC) grid 7. Remove Small Arms Collimator (SAC)

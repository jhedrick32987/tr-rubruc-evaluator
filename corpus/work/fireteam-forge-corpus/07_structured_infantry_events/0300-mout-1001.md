# 0300-MOUT-1001 Perform Individual Movement in an Urban Environment

## Evaluation coded

NO SUSTAINMENT INTERVAL: 12 months

## Readiness coded

NO

## MOS performing

0300, 0302, 0306, 0307, 0311, 0317, 0321, 0331, 0341, 0351, 0352, 0365, 0369

## Grades

PVT, PFC, LCPL, CPL, SGT, SSGT, GYSGT, 2NDLT, 1STLT, CAPT

## Initial training setting

FORMAL

## Condition

Given an assigned weapon and a mission, while wearing a fighting load.

## Standard

To reduce exposure to enemy fire. (MCRP 12-10B.1, Military Operations on Urbanized Terrain, Appendix A, Section III, pg. A-7)

## Performance Steps

1. Cross a wall 2. Observe around a corner 3. Move past a window 4. Move past a door 5. Move parallel to a building 6. Cross a danger area 7. Conduct cross cover during movement

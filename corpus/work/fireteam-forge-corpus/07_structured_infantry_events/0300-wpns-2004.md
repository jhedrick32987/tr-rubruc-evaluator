# 0300-WPNS-2004 Engage Targets with an AT-4 Light Anti-armor Weapon

## Evaluation coded

NO SUSTAINMENT INTERVAL: 12 months

## Readiness coded

NO

## MOS performing

## Grades

PVT, PFC, LCPL, CPL, SGT, SSGT, GYSGT, MSGT, 2NDLT, 1STLT, CAPT

## Initial training setting

MOJT

## Condition

Given an AT-4 light anti-armor weapon and a target, while wearing a fighting load.

## Standard

To achieve a hit on target.

## Performance Steps

1. Inspect the weapon 2. Prepare the weapon for firing 3. Estimate range to target 4. Ensure the sights are adjusted for the correct range of the target 5. Visually inspect and clear the back blast area 6. Engage the target with the weapon 7. Dispose of expended tube

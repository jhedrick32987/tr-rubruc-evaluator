## Condition

Given a loaded Multi-Role, Anti-Armor, Anti-Personnel Weapon System (MAAWS), while wearing a fighting load.

## Standard

To make the weapon Condition-4.

## Performance Steps

1. Hold the weapon as when loading 2. Push cocking lever fully forward with right hand thumb 3. Set Safety catch to S (SAFE). 4. Order "UNLOAD". 5. Using right hand, move venturi locking lever forward and open venturi 6. Flick venturi locking lever forward, thus forcing round rearwards 7. Remove round from weapon with left hand and put it down 8. Using right hand, close venturi NAVMC 3500.44D 07 May 2020

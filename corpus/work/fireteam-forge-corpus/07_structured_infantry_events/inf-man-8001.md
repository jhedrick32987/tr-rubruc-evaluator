## Description

The offense is the decisive form of warfare. While other operations may do great damage to, or harm to the interests of, an enemy or adversary, offensive operations are the means to decisive victory. An offensive operation is an operation conducted to take the initiative from the enemy, gain freedom of action, and generate effects to achieve objectives. The four types of offensive operations are movement to contact, attack, exploitation, and pursuit. (MCWP 3-01, Offensive and Defensive Tactics, Ch. 3)

## Condition

Given supporting attachments operating in a Marine air-ground task force (MAGTF), joint, combined, and/or interagency environment, a higher headquarters' operations order, and commander's guidance.

## Standard

To allow the commander to impose his will on the enemy by shattering the enemy's moral, mental, and physical cohesion. (MCDP 1, Warfighting)

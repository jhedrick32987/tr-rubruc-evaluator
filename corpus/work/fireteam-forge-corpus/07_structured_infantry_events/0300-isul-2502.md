Weaponeering is defined by "selecting the best supporting arms to attack the target considering availability and coordination requirements, weaponeering is selecting the appropriate weapon and munitions based on the desired effect and lethality and non-lethality of a specific munition" MCRP 3-31.5 MTTP for Dynamic Targeting. Targeting is the process of selecting targets and matching the appropriate response to them comprised of the following four activities: Decide, Detect, Deliver, and Assess (D3A).

# 0317-MARK-2010 Operate a Firing Solution Device

## Evaluation coded

NO SUSTAINMENT INTERVAL: 6 months

## Readiness coded

NO

## MOS performing

0317

## Grades

PFC, LCPL, CPL, SGT

## Initial training setting

FORMAL

## Condition

Given a zeroed sniper rifle, data book, firing solution device, observer, 20 rounds of ammunition, spotting scope, a target at transonic range of a given weapon system and wearing the directed body armor protection level.

## Standard

To process data that will facilitate point of aim/point of impact within the first round.

## Performance Steps

1. Inspect device and components 2. Install batteries 3. Calibrate device 4. Program constants: a. Muzzle velocity b. Drag curve c. Ballistic coefficient d. Bullet dimensions e. Zeroing range f. Bore height g. Barrel rate of twist 5. Enter weapon specific data into ballistic program 6. Analyze firing solution 7. Apply firing solution to the sniper weapon system 8. Trouble shoot device, as required 9. Perform maintenance, as required

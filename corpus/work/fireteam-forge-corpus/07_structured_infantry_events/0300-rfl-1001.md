# 0300-RFL-1001 Perform Weapons Handling Procedures

## Evaluation coded

NO SUSTAINMENT INTERVAL: 12 months

## Readiness coded

NO

## MOS performing

0300, 0302, 0306, 0307, 0311, 0321, 0331, 0341, 0351, 0352, 0365, 0369

## Grades

PVT, PFC, LCPL, CPL, SGT, SSGT, GYSGT, 2NDLT, 1STLT, CAPT

## Initial training setting

FORMAL

## Condition

Given a service rifle, sling, magazines, individual equipment, and ammunition.

## Standard

To handle, operate, and employ the Service rifle. (MCRP 8-10B.2 Rifle Marksmanship, Ch. 4, pg. 4-1)

## Performance Steps

1. Follow the four safety rules 2. Assemble and adjust of the Common Weapon Sling 3. Demonstrate the four weapon carries 4. Fill magazines 5. Take a weapon from Condition-4 to Condition-1 6. Take a weapon from Condition-1 to Condition-4 7. Perform reloads 8. Perform corrective actions for stoppages and malfunctions

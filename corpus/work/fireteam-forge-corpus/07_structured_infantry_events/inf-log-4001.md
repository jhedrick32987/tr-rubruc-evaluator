## Description

The platoon plans to process and evacuate detainees and captured enemy materiel as part of the operation order. Evacuating large numbers of detainees is normally done using logistical assets, but attention is paid to maintaining security and a chain of custody, and preserving items of intelligence value.

## Condition

Given the apprehension of personnel during operations.

## Standard

To ensure safe and expeditious handling of detainees in accordance with the laws of armed conflict.

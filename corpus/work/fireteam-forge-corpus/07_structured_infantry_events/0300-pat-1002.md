# 0300-PAT-1002 Navigate with a Map and Compass

## Evaluation coded

NO SUSTAINMENT INTERVAL: 12 months

## Readiness coded

NO

## MOS performing

0300, 0302, 0311, 0317, 0331, 0341, 0351, 0352, 0365, 0369

## Grades

PVT, PFC, LCPL, CPL, SGT, SSGT, GYSGT, 2NDLT, 1STLT, CAPT

## Initial training setting

FORMAL

## Condition

Given periods of daylight or darkness, a lensatic compass, map, and designated objectives, while wearing a fighting load.

## Standard

To arrive at each designated objective.

## Performance Steps

1. Plan a navigation route 2. Determine pace count for different terrain 3. Orient the map. 4. Adjust compass for conditions 5. Follow azimuth for set distance 6. Perform deliberate offset, as required 7. Perform two point resection, as required 8. Report objectives, as required

## Condition

## Standard

To direct a breaching operation

## Performance Steps

1. Task organize unit a. Breach element b. Support element 2. Analyze the situation a. Obstacle b. Enemy situation c. Terrain d. Breach point e. Available breaching assets f. Supporting arms 3. Establish security of the breach site 4. Suppress the enemy 5. Direct obscuration of the breach site 6. Direct the breach element to the breach point 7. Reduce the obstacle 8. Proof and mark the lane 9. Establish security beyond the breach point 10. Move all remaining elements through the breach lane 11. Report location of breach lane to higher headquarters 12. Request additional assets based on METT-T a. Mission b. Enemy c. Terrain and weather d. Troops and fire support available e. Time 13. Plan for redundancy in breaching assets

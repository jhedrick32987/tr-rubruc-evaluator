## Description

The 81-mm mortar platoon provides responsive indirect fires that support company/battalion level maneuver elements in the offense and reinforce direct fires in the defense. The platoon can operate mounted or dismounted, as a massed platoon, or as two independent sections.

## Condition

Given a mission, a commander's intent, and a supported unit(s) scheme of maneuver

## Standard

Support unit's scheme of maneuver and achieve desired effects on the enemy in accordance with the commander's intent.

## Administrative Instructions

This event applies to supporting offensive, defensive, patrolling, and urban operations as well as conducted with or without vehicle assets. Tactical logistics for mortar live fire is pre-staging and combat loading vehicles/packs with ammo as well as coordinating tactical resupply. Split section/platoon considerations include tactical fire direction, communication deconfliction for conduct of fire nets/command and control net, echelon of movement, logistics, overwatch, firing positions, etc. in support of the unit(s) scheme of maneuver. {45FCA6B1-D18E-4114-E053-02C16ECD9283}*{8e8e5a52-df42-4fc1-aee4-4fdd35b5afdf}

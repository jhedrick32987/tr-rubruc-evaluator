## Condition

## Standard

## Performance Steps

1. Place the charge on target 2. Prime charge 3. From target to detonation point, inspect firing wire for serviceability 4. Take cover 5. Test firing wire 6. Connect firing wire to firing device 7. On order, initiate charge

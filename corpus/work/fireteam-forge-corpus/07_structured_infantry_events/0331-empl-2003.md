## Evaluation coded

## Initial training setting

FORMAL

## Condition

Given a map, paper, pencil, gun position, and an objective

## Standard

To determine the dead space or mask along the gun target line. (MCTP 3-01C, Machine Guns and Machine Gun Gunnery, Ch. 6, p. 6-59)

## Performance Steps

1. Locate both the gun and target position on a map 2. Position the straight edge of a piece of paper along the direction of fire 3. Annotate the elevation of each contour line 4. Draw perpendicular lines down to its corresponding elevation point 5. Connect each perpendicular lines utilizing smooth curves

# 0306-OPS-2005 Develop a Range

## Evaluation coded

NO SUSTAINMENT INTERVAL: 12 months

## Readiness coded

NO

## MOS performing

0306

## Grades

CWO-2, CWO-3, CWO-4, CWO-5

## Initial training setting

FORMAL

## Condition

Given a requirement to establish a live fire range

## Standard

To ensure the range meets DA-Pam 385-63 standards.

## Performance Steps

1. Identify possible range sites. 2. Incorporate surface danger zone/weapon danger zone (SDZ/WDZ) for DODIC to be fired 3. Verify the range meets certification requirements and commander's intent 4. Develop range regulations 5. Present an alternate solution to resolve shortfalls

## Primary Reference

DA PAM 385-63 Range Safety

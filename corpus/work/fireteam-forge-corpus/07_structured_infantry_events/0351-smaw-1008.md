# 0351-SMAW-1008 Unload a MK153 SMAW

## Evaluation coded

NO SUSTAINMENT INTERVAL: 6 months

## Readiness coded

NO

## MOS performing

0351

## Billets

Gunner, Squad Leader, Team Leader

## Grades

PVT, PFC, LCPL, CPL

## Initial training setting

FORMAL

## Condition

Given a condition (1) MK153 SMAW, an encased rocket or an expended rocket, and a magazine of spotting rounds, while wearing a fighting load.

## Standard

To make the weapon condition (4).

## Performance Steps

1. Ensure the weapon is on safe 2. Remove magazine from the spotting rifle 3. Physically and visually inspect spotting rifle 4. Assume the load position 5. Remove rocket and immediately replace forward end cap 6. Point launcher in a safe direction, depress launch lever/trigger, and place the weapon on safe (as needed)

## Performance Steps

NAVMC 3500.44D 07 May 2020

9-47 Enclosure (1) 1. Lead unit in response to enemy contact 2. Disseminate fire commands to the unit using (ADDRAC) a. Alert b. Direction c. Description d. Range e. Assignment f. Control 3. Establish base of fire to gain fire superiority 4. Determine effectiveness of fires relative to the enemy 5. Transmit Situation Report (SITREP) to higher 6. Determine courses of action available to unit in relation to terrain, enemy, and adjacent units 7. Execute appropriate actions in accordance with unit capabilities and assessment of the situation 8. Report to higher actions or recommendations

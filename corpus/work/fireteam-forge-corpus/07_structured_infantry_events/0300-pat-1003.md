# 0300-PAT-1003 Prepare for Combat

## Evaluation coded

NO SUSTAINMENT INTERVAL: 12 months

## Readiness coded

NO

## MOS performing

0300, 0302, 0311, 0317, 0331, 0341, 0351, 0352, 0365, 0369

## Grades

PVT, PFC, LCPL, CPL, SGT, SSGT, GYSGT, 2NDLT, 1STLT, CAPT

## Initial training setting

FORMAL NAVMC 3500.44D 07 May 2020

8-17 Enclosure (1)

## Condition

Given an order with a mission to conduct combat operations, while wearing a fighting load.

## Standard

To ensure the individual is prepared to accomplish the mission.

## Performance Steps

1. Receive a warning order 2. Conduct preparations per the warning order 3. Receive an operations order 4. Participate in rehearsals 5. Conduct pre-combat checks (PCCs) 6. Stand pre-combat inspections (PCIs) 7. Test fire individual weapon, when feasible

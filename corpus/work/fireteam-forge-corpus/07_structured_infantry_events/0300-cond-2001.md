# 0300-COND-2001 Conduct a Forced March

## Evaluation coded

## Readiness coded

NO

## MOS performing

PVT, PFC, LCPL, CPL, SGT, SSGT, GYSGT, MSGT, 2NDLT, 1STLT, CAPT

## Initial training setting

MOJT

## Condition

Given an assault load of 70 pounds (+/- 10%), assigned weapon and mission essential equipment.

## Standard

To move 32 kilometers in a time limit of 8 hours.

1. Prepare for combat 2. Complete a 32 kilometer march 3. Conduct follow-on actions

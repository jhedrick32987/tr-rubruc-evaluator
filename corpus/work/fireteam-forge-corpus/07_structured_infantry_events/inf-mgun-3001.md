## Description

Crew qualification is for guns crews that have demonstrated proficiency during the basic phases of gunnery. It is designed to increase the effectiveness of the machinegun crew by building their confidence to quickly and accurately deliver a large volume of fire on a predetermined target. Crew qualification is required in order to qualify a machinegun crew to support maneuver in a platoon or larger collective event.

## Condition

Given a machinegun crew, a machinegun gun with T&E and SL-3, either ground mounted on a tripod or vehicle mounted, during day and limited visibility.

## Standard

To qualify a machinegun crew.

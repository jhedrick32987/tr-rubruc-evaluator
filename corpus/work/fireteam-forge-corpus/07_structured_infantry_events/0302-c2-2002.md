# 0302-C2-2002 Perform Duties in the Marine Corps Planning Process (MCPP)

## Evaluation coded

NO

## Sustainment interval

6 months

## Readiness coded

NO

## MOS performing

0302

## Grades

CAPT, MAJ, LTCOL

## Initial training setting

FORMAL

## Condition

Given a higher commander's order, planning guidance, and subject matter experts.

## Standard

To support the commander's decision making and develop a concept of operations.

## Performance Steps

1. Conduct problem framing 2. Develop course(s) of action 3. War game course(s) of action 4. Compare courses of action, then present for commander's decision 5. Develop orders 6. Provide transition briefs to key personnel

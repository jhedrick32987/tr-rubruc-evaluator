Identify targets by precedence. 5. Establish engagement criteria 6. Issue fire commands 7. Direct target engagement 8. Improve positions, as necessary 9. Execute the screening and signal plan 10. Determine the effects on target(s) 11. Re-engage target(s), as necessary 12. Displace, as necessary
